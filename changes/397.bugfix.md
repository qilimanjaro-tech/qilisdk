QiliSim now handles noise acting on controls correctly: previously if you had noise on qubit 1 and then did a CNOT controlled on qubit 1, the noise would not apply since it only checked for the targets of the gate, not the controls.

Also fixed qubit-specific noise not spreading to all targets of a multi-target gate: previously if one did noise on qubit 1 and then did a swap between qubits 1 and 2, the noise would applied to both 1 and 2, but it should only be applied to 1 since it's qubit specific.
