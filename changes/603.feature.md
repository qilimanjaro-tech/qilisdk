It's now easier to calculate the minimum gap of a schedule (a critical metric determining how slow you need to go in the anneal), via a new function: `Schedule.minimum_gap()`. Performance:

| Qubits | Time     |
|--------|----------|
| 10     | 0.54 s   |
| 11     | 1.09 s   |
| 12     | 3.12 s   |
| 13     | 11.42 s  |
| 14     | 34.25 s  |
