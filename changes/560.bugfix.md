`ObservableCostFunction` now handles qubit indices better in the case where you give it a Hamiltonian or Pauli. Previously if you gave it a Pauli it would convert it to a 2x2 QTensor and thus when you actually used it the index that you gave to the Pauli will be lost. Now Paulis and Hamiltonians keep their indices rather than converting to QTensor. We now use expect_val in both cost functions so everything is unified.
