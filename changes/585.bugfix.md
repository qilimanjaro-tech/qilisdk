The Lindbladian form of `PauliChannel` has been made more consistent with its Kraus operator form. Previously the two were out of sync, as the Lindblad form was only calculating rates up to first order. The conversion to Lindblad form also now raises errors/warnings for certain `PauliChannels` that don't have exact Lindblad forms (e.g. a `BitFlip` with probability 0.7).
